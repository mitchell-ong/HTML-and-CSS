Whiskers of Fury
By Mitchell Ong and ChatGPT

In the world of Felidale and Canidra, the lands of cats and dogs, peace was once the stuff of bedtime stories. 
Long ago, the Great Treaty kept the balance between claw and fang. But as generations passed, mistrust grew, 
and the Treaty crumbled into dust and memory.

Amid this simmering conflict, a lone kitten was found curled on the cold stone steps of the Silent Paw Monastery, 
nestled deep in the Misty Claw Mountains. The monks—ancient feline warriors trained in the art of Meow-Fu—
found the infant mewling, eyes barely open, a scrap of fur and vulnerability. He had no name, no memory, no kin. 
But Master Shifu, the oldest and wisest of the kung-fu masters, saw something in the kitten’s trembling gaze: fire.

They named him Mao, after the old word for "courage in silence."

Years passed like falling leaves. While the other initiates were born of noble cat bloodlines and trained from birth, 
Mao struggled. He was smaller. Weaker. But never once did he quit. He endured endless drills, claw discipline, 
balance exercises atop swaying bamboo, and the painful silence of being the outsider.

Yet in time, his flaws became strengths. His small size taught him agility. His hunger taught him patience. 
His silence made his strikes thunder.

But the world outside the monastery was changing. The Hound Regime, led by General Ironjaw, had begun a campaign 
of conquest across the lowlands. Rumors swirled that the dogs were seeking ancient cat scrolls—
secrets of Meow-Fu, hidden in the monastery’s vaults.

And when the storm finally came, it would fall on the paws of an orphaned cat to decide the fate of both species.
